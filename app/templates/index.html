How optimization works

The score combines distance, priority delay, and minutes outside delivery windows. For up to 8 stops, the planner evaluates every order. For larger lists, it starts from a nearest-stop route and improves it using 2-opt and stop relocation. Every route starts and ends at the warehouse.

OSRM uses the public OSRM table service and OpenStreetMap road data to return driving distance and expected duration. It is not live traffic data. Straight-line mode uses your average-speed estimate. Time windows use same-day 24-hour times; waiting is allowed before a window opens. Late arrivals add the displayed penalty. Coordinates and time windows are validated.
